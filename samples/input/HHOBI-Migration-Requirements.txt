HHOBI Data Migration - Source Requirements Notes
Document ID: CG-DOC-98178
Author: Business Analysis Team
Date: July 2026

PROJECT OVERVIEW
The HHOBI application currently runs on a legacy platform. The business wants to migrate it to the Federated Data Lake (FDL) platform to improve scalability, maintainability, and reliability of data processing.

BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
- Migrate the HHOBI application from the legacy platform to FDL.
- Reduce operational overhead associated with maintaining legacy infrastructure.
- Standardize data ingestion, transformation, and consumption using enterprise FDL architecture.
- Enable future onboarding of additional source systems with minimal development effort.
- Maintain uninterrupted reporting and extract generation during and after migration.

CURRENT STATE
- Data is ingested from multiple source systems.
- Consumption is mainly through reports and extracts.
- Legacy processes are difficult to scale and maintain.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
1. The migration must ingest data from multiple sources and process it for downstream consumption.
2. Reporting and extract generation must continue during and after migration.
3. Product information must be sourced from the IA_PRODUCTS table.
4. Customer information must be sourced from the IA_CUSTOMERS table.
5. Mandatory attributes must be available before records are loaded into curated datasets.
6. Duplicate records must be identified and handled per data governance standards.
7. Only valid source records shall be processed.

NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- Improve scalability, maintainability, and reliability of data processing.
- Maintain data quality, consistency, and compliance throughout migration.
- Database must support ACID compliance, backup and recovery, and performance optimization based on data volume.

ASSUMPTIONS
- Source systems will remain available during migration planning.
- FDL platform capacity will be provisioned before cutover.

CONSTRAINTS
- Only valid source records shall be processed.
- Migration must not disrupt existing report consumers.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Reports and extracts work without interruption during and after migration.
- Curated datasets contain mandatory attributes before load.
- Duplicate handling follows data governance standards.

STAKEHOLDERS
- Business Owner: project sponsorship and approvals.
- Business Analyst: requirements and UAT support.
- Development Team: design, build, and unit testing.
- QA Team: system and regression testing.
- End Users: acceptance testing and feedback.

RISKS
- Technical complexity of multi-source ingestion.
- Scope creep from additional source onboarding requests.
- Resource constraints during parallel legacy and FDL operations.
- Integration challenges with existing report consumers.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
Suggested phases: Planning, Design, Development, Testing, Deployment.
Timeline to be finalized after requirements sign-off.
